The 9-Step HealthNest Pipeline

End-to-end flow from raw Kaggle Q&A corpus to sentence-level verified answers.

  • 1
    CSV Dataset Ingestion & Validation

    Flexible schema reader loading ~16,000 Q&A pairs from Kaggle MedQuAD without external dependencies.

  • 2
    Document Chunking & Token Granularity

    Sentence-level chunk windowing with metadata tracking to establish discrete retrieval units.

  • 3
    Text Normalization & Hinglish Expansion

    Devanagari regex matching, lowercase normalization, Porter stemmer, and 60+ Hinglish symptom phrase expansions.

  • 4
    Hand-Written Multi-Zone Inverted Index

    Weighted zone indexing (Title weight 2.5, Body weight 1.0) with postings lists mapping doc IDs and term frequencies.

  • 5
    SMART lnc.ltc TF-IDF Vector Space Model

    Logarithmic term weighting, inverse document frequency ln(N/df), and cosine document length normalization.

  • 6
    Dense Embedding Semantic Retrieval

    Paraphrase-multilingual MiniLM embeddings with pure NumPy cosine dot-product similarity ranking.

  • 7
    Reciprocal Rank Fusion (Hybrid RRF)

    Unsupervised hybrid fusion formula combining sparse keyword rank and dense semantic rank with constant k=60.

  • 8
    Selective Abstention Protocol

    Evidence confidence thresholding with fallback: explicitly answering "I don't know based on the provided pages" when evidence is weak.

  • 9
    Sentence-Level Fact Verification Checker

    Independent verification verifying each generated sentence against context citations using sparse cosine similarity.
